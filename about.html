About

Measure first.

Recallant started in May 2026 with one question: when buyers ask AI what to buy, who gets recommended, and why?

Why Recallant exists

Buyers used to search, click and compare. Now many ask an assistant and read one answer. That answer usually names three or four products. If yours isn’t one of them, you never hear about the deal you lost.

Most advice about “AI SEO” is guesswork. We start by measuring: the same buyer questions, asked many times, with every answer and every cited source recorded. Then we work on what the data shows, and measure again.

How we work

  • Evidence over opinion. Every recommendation points to the answers and sources behind it.
  • Honest limits. We say which assistants we measured, when, and what we didn’t measure.
  • Nothing fake. No bought reviews, links or mentions, ever.
  • Plain reporting. One page you can read in two minutes, not a dashboard you have to decode.
