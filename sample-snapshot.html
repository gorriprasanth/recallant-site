Where you’re named

Freshdesk is the most-named helpdesk, with the largest share of brand mentions (19%, against Zendesk’s 14%). It was named first in all 5 runs of these questions:

Which pages AI relies on

Answers cite listicles and other vendors’ blogs far more than Freshdesk’s own pages. The sites cited most, counted as answers out of 220:

On how-to questions, hubspot.com was cited in 20 of 50 answers, and freshworks.com in 9.

What to fix first

  1. Answer the niche questions directly.Check whether ecommerce support, shared inboxes or AI chatbots match the product’s actual strengths. Where they do, review whether its pages answer those questions clearly. The study does not establish why a brand is absent.
  2. Own the how-to questions.Review practical guides on SLAs, routing and WhatsApp support against product capabilities. HubSpot’s blog was frequently cited in this dated sample.
  3. Fix the third-party lists.Make sure G2 and the most-cited comparison articles describe Freshdesk accurately.
