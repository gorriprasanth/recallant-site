What to fix first

  1. Answer the niche questions directly. Publish pages for ecommerce support, shared inboxes and AI chatbots. Rivals win these because their pages match the question.
  2. Own the how-to questions. Practical guides on SLAs, routing and WhatsApp support, where HubSpot's blog is cited most today.
  3. Fix the third-party lists. Make sure G2 and the most-cited comparison articles describe Freshdesk accurately.
