How the work would unfold

Agree the evidence

Define the audience, question set, platform, collection dates and supplied captures. Keep the exact questions and saved answers attached to the analysis. Fresh answer collection is separately scoped.

Separate the signals

Record a brand mention, first-named position, citation and affirmative recommendation separately. Keep the denominator and missing captures visible; do not convert a mention into an endorsement.

Investigate before prescribing

Inspect cited pages and the vendor’s relevant pages. Distinguish observed patterns from hypotheses. Prioritise a supported page improvement or an evidence gap that needs further investigation.

Inside the work

A finding that can be checked

Observation
Record exactly what the saved answer says, with its question, date and source capture.
Interpretation
Explain what it may mean for this buying question. Mark uncertainty explicitly.
Next action
Review the relevant comparison page and its evidence before recommending an edit.
