The method

Keep the question.
Keep the evidence.

A useful visibility finding lets you inspect what was asked, what came back and how the conclusion was reached.

Read the measures correctly

Different signals. Different meanings.

Brand mention

The answer names the brand under the study’s stated matching rules. A mention can occur in a comparison, a criticism or a passing reference.

Source citation

The answer links to a retained source. An owned-domain citation and a third-party citation are separate observations; neither alone proves endorsement.

First-named order

The first tracked brand named under the study’s exclusions. This is an order proxy, not an affirmative recommendation or verified preference.

Business outcomes

Traffic, qualified enquiries and revenue need their own evidence and definitions. They cannot be inferred from a mention, citation or first-named position.

From sample to decision

Make every finding inspectable.

Agree the question and the surface

Define the brand, competitors, exact questions, market, language, collection dates and permitted source. API results and consumer-app observations are recorded separately. An API response does not establish what every person will see in the consumer app.

Preserve the answer and the coverage

Retain the question, capture date, answer reference and source links. Count usable, failed and missing captures separately. A missing capture is not an answer that omitted the brand.

Show the denominator

A raw count such as “two of five saved answers” and a weighted aggregate answer different questions. Report the unit, eligible population and weighting. Share of voice is relative to the brands tracked; it is not market share.

Separate observation from interpretation

A changed question may produce a different answer. That observation does not by itself explain the cause or prove that rewriting a page will change the result. Recommendations to improve a page should state the evidence, the proposed action and what needs checking.

Compare like with like

For repeat work, keep the agreed question set and method consistent or explain what changed. Preserve the first report and issue dated corrections. A movement between samples does not establish that one edit caused it.

Read the public study in context
