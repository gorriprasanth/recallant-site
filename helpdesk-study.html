How to read these figures

This is evidence collected on 6 October 2026. “First named” records naming order among tracked brands; it does not establish an affirmative recommendation. Headline rates use the study’s weighting. Own-site citation rates apply to eligible answers with inline citations, while source counts use the denominator printed beside them. Measurement definitions →

On Perplexity's live-search answers, Freshdesk is the most-named helpdesk (named in 61% of answers to buyer questions) and leads share of voice (19% against Zendesk's 14%, with overlapping ranges). But Zendesk is named first more often, and Freshdesk's own pages are cited less often than Help Scout's. A 10-question spot check in the ChatGPT app found rivals recommended ahead of Freshdesk on the small-business (Help Scout) and India (Zoho Desk) questions. Based on 220 Perplexity answers (44 buyer questions, 5 runs each) collected on 6 Oct 2026.

Who gets cited

Perplexity mostly cites listicles and other vendors' blogs, not Freshdesk's own pages. Each answer cited about 5 sources.

  • Own pages: Freshworks' site was cited in 13% of non-branded answers. Help Scout's was cited in 21%, and Intercom's, Zendesk's and HubSpot's in 14–15%. Help Scout, Intercom and HubSpot are cited far more often than they are named first, which means their content does the work.
  • How-to questions: hubspot.com was cited in 20 of the 50 how-to answers and freshworks.com in 9. HubSpot's blog owns the "how do I" questions even though HubSpot is rarely recommended.

What this means

A brand can be recommended often and still be cited rarely, and every gap above can be measured month by month. For any helpdesk brand, the openings are:

  1. Niche questions: write pages that answer the ecommerce, shared-inbox, SaaS-startup and AI-chatbot questions directly. Review whether the relevant product pages answer these questions. Naming order alone does not establish the cause.
  2. How-to questions: publish practical guides on SLAs, routing, knowledge bases and WhatsApp support. HubSpot's blog wins these now, and most of these answers name no product yet.
  3. Third-party lists: earn accurate listings on the review sites and comparison blogs Perplexity cites, starting with G2, Capterra and the most-cited vendor listicles. Do this through outreach and corrections, never paid or fake reviews.
  4. Re-measure: run the same 44 questions every month. The prompt set stays fixed, so changes can be tested for significance.

  • The first recommendation matched Perplexity on 7 of the 10 questions.
  • The differences hurt Freshdesk. On the two broad questions it wins on Perplexity, ChatGPT recommends a rival first: Help Scout for small business (Freshdesk 3rd) and Zoho Desk for India (Freshdesk 2nd).
  • ChatGPT still named Freshdesk in 8 of 10 answers, usually as the value or scale-up option rather than the first pick.
  • ChatGPT said Freshdesk's permanent free plan has been replaced by a trial. That is accurate. Freshdesk's support article (updated 22 Sep 2026) describes a Free program that lasts 6 months for up to two agents, and its pricing page lists only paid plans and a 14-day trial (both checked 6 Oct 2026). So on "free plan" questions, Freshdesk loses to Zoho Desk and Help Scout on the facts, not because of an AI error.

Limits: one answer per question, from one ChatGPT Plus account in India, so some prices came in rupees.

How it was measured and its limits

These are Perplexity results only. They are not "AI search" in general.

  • Engine: Perplexity's Agent API with the fast preset (model openai/gpt-6-luna) and live web search. The Perplexity app can answer differently from the API.
  • Questions: 44 buyer questions:
    • 16 "best for" questions, weighted double
    • 8 "alternatives to" questions
    • 6 comparisons, 5 of them between two rivals
    • 10 how-to questions
    • 4 about Freshdesk itself, kept out of share of voice
  • Runs: each question was asked 5 times on 6 Oct 2026. The answers barely varied between runs, so later studies should ask more questions and run each one fewer times.
  • Ranges: 95% ranges come from resampling the questions (bootstrap).
  • Brands: 23 brands are matched by name and alias, whatever the tone of the mention. Thirteen of them were added after reading the answers. freshworks.com counts as Freshdesk's own site, although it also hosts Freshservice pages.
  • Brands named in the question: when a question names a brand ("alternatives to Zendesk", "Zendesk vs Intercom"), that brand is left out of that question's numbers, because repeating the question's own words is not visibility.
  • Not measured: ChatGPT (apart from the 10-answer spot check above), Gemini, Claude and Google AI Overviews. These were left out for budget reasons, and Gemini's free tier allows only 20 requests a day.
  • Questions asked: all 44 are listed word for word in the appendix below.
