How we measure

The same questions, asked many times.

Every study and every client report follows the same four rules.

  1. 01

    Fix the questions

    We write the questions buyers ask, in four groups: best-for, alternatives, comparisons and how-to. The set stays the same from month to month.

  2. 02

    Ask more than once

    Each question is asked several times, so one lucky or unlucky answer doesn’t decide the result.

  3. 03

    Read every answer

    Every brand mention, first recommendation and cited source is recorded. A brand named in the question is left out of that question’s numbers.

  4. 04

    Show the uncertainty

    Results come with 95% ranges, and we publish the method and its limits.
