Privacy

A clear route
for your enquiry.

Recallant is part of Signal & Syllable. This page explains what happens when you browse this website or contact us.

Browsing recallant.com

Shared links can carry four short labels for their source, link type, campaign and content reference. With JavaScript available, valid labels follow page links on this website and the four Recallant enquiry choices into the shared studio form. We do not save them in cookies or browser storage, collect a full referring URL for enquiry attribution. Optional measurement is described below. The form shows the labels so you can clear them before reviewing and sending your enquiry.

This is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. We have not added advertising trackers, session recording or a local enquiry form to this version. The research explorer works in your browser with the published study data; choosing a question does not send us your contact details.

The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP address, browser details and access logs, to serve and protect the website. Its handling is described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Using the shared enquiry desk

Enquiry buttons take you to signalandsyllable.com, where Signal & Syllable operates the shared form. The link passes your selected Recallant interest; it does not submit personal information from this website.

On that form, the contact details and project information you choose to submit are processed through Cloudflare hosting and AgentMail email delivery, then sent to our receiving email account. The form includes a review step and requires an explicit send action. Read the Signal & Syllable privacy notice before submitting.

Contacting us by email

An email link opens your email application. Sending your message shares its contents and address with our receiving email service at hello@recallant.com. We use an enquiry to respond and discuss the requested work; it does not subscribe you to marketing updates.

What to share

Use public links and a brief description of your business question. Do not send passwords, account keys, private customer records or confidential documents through the initial enquiry route. Any additional material needed for an engagement is agreed separately.

Optional site measurement

Cloudflare Web Analytics measures page visits and loading performance only after you choose Allow measurement. It does not use advertising cookies or session recordings. We do not load its script on the enquiry page or send form contents to it. Page, device, browser, approximate country and performance information may be processed by Cloudflare; hosting request logs are separate and operate regardless of this choice.

Your allow/off preference is stored in this browser’s local storage. Use Measurement preferences in the footer to change it. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals keep optional measurement off. Blocking storage can prevent your choice from persisting. Counts cover only participating browsers and are not a count of customers, qualified enquiries or revenue. See Cloudflare’s measurement documentation.

Questions or corrections

For questions about an enquiry, or to request a correction or deletion, contact hello@recallant.com. Include enough information for us to identify your request without resending sensitive material. Handling follows the shared enquiry privacy notice linked above.

Booking a conversation

Our optional booking link opens Zoho Bookings. Zoho processes the name, email address, optional contact number and appointment details you provide to manage your booking, send confirmations and create a Zoho Meeting video link. We use these details to arrange your conversation. You can cancel from your confirmation email. Opening the booking link does not submit an enquiry. See Zoho’s privacy policy.

Other websites

Research sources, LinkedIn and other external links have their own privacy practices. Clicking those links takes you to the relevant provider’s website.

Price display preference

Cloudflare estimates your country from the connection IP to suggest an initial display currency. Our currency endpoint returns only that currency, does not store the IP or country, and is not cached. Your saved selection takes priority; you can change it at any time. The currency selector remembers your choice for this tab in browser session storage. Estimated conversions use dated reference rates on your device, with no visitor request to an exchange-rate provider. INR remains the base price; the proposal confirms billing currency.
