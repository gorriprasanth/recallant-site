Privacy

A clear route
for your enquiry.

Recallant is part of Signal & Syllable. This page explains what happens when you browse this website or contact us.

Browsing recallant.com

Shared links can carry four short labels for their source, link type, campaign and content reference. With JavaScript available, valid labels follow page links on this website and the four Recallant enquiry choices into the shared studio form. We do not save them in cookies or browser storage, collect a full referring URL or add visitor analytics. The form shows the labels so you can clear them before reviewing and sending your enquiry.

This is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics, advertising trackers or a local enquiry form to this version. The research explorer works in your browser with the published study data; choosing a question does not send us your contact details.

The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP address, browser details and access logs, to serve and protect the website. Its handling is described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Using the shared enquiry desk

Enquiry buttons take you to signalandsyllable.com, where Signal & Syllable operates the shared form. The link passes your selected Recallant interest; it does not submit personal information from this website.

On that form, the contact details and project information you choose to submit are processed through Cloudflare hosting and AgentMail email delivery, then sent to our receiving email account. The form includes a review step and requires an explicit send action. Read the Signal & Syllable privacy notice before submitting.

Contacting us by email

An email link opens your email application. Sending your message shares its contents and address with our receiving email service at gorripatiprasanth@gmail.com. We use an enquiry to respond and discuss the requested work; it does not subscribe you to marketing updates.

What to share

Use public links and a brief description of your business question. Do not send passwords, account keys, private customer records or confidential documents through the initial enquiry route. Any additional material needed for an engagement is agreed separately.

Questions or corrections

For questions about an enquiry, or to request a correction or deletion, contact gorripatiprasanth@gmail.com. Include enough information for us to identify your request without resending sensitive material. Handling follows the shared enquiry privacy notice linked above.

Other websites

Research sources, LinkedIn and other external links have their own privacy practices. Clicking those links takes you to the relevant provider’s website.
