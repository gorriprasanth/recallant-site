The work

Visibility review

See the evidence behind the answer.

Start with the questions a buyer would actually ask. Inspect brand mentions, linked sources and first-named order across the agreed sample.

  • A defined question set, market and competitor list
  • Agreed answer surfaces, collection method and dates
  • Separate observations for mentions and citations
  • A record of usable, missing and failed captures

Priority improvements

Give the buyer a clearer decision.

Translate supported findings into a focused action brief. Review the pages, product facts and buyer questions that matter before choosing what to write.

  • Prioritised findings with the evidence alongside
  • Buyer-question and page mapping

Agreed follow-up

Compare changes on a consistent basis.

When repeat measurement is useful, agree the questions, cadence and source of each comparison. Keep edits and changes in the sample visible.

  • A repeatable, agreed measurement scope
  • Comparable periods and clearly labelled exceptions
  • A change log for reviewed content and interpretations
  • A practical review of the next priorities

Built around your scope

A useful question comes first.

Recallant is for B2B founders and marketing teams with a clear product, an identifiable buyer and someone who can act on the findings. Start with one decision: a shortlist, a comparison, a product-fit question or a useful demo enquiry.

The platform, API or consumer-app surface, language, sample size and collection method are confirmed before work begins. Supplied saved answers and fresh collection are different scopes. Repeat measurement and implementation are agreed separately.

Website projects: Pagecraft covers separately scoped website work.

Positioning: Signal Map helps clarify your message and buyer decision.
