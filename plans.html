The pilot, in detail

A clear beginning. A useful handover.

Starting material
Your public domain, intended buyer, the decision to improve and confirmed product facts. For answer visibility, supply up to 25 authorised saved captures across at most five agreed questions, including dates and sources.
Without saved answers
The pilot covers the agreed website pages. It does not report an AI-answer visibility baseline without the required evidence.
Timing
Target first delivery is five working days after complete intake and a confirmed start date. Timing and terms are confirmed in the proposal.
Revision
One consolidated round of factual corrections and clarity changes to the agreed scope, requested within seven calendar days of delivery. A different audience, page or offer needs a revised scope.
Separate work
Fresh AI sampling, design/build work, publishing, ongoing monitoring, analytics setup, paid tools, outreach and additional domains or languages are not included in this pilot.

The handover links each finding to its source and makes the proposed change clear. A visibility review does not guarantee rankings, citations, recommendations, enquiries or revenue.
